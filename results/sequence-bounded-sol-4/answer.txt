**Turns get disposable working directories, but those directories alone are not a filesystem security boundary.** Your two-debater, no-judge deployment remains unchanged; judge behavior below describes the optional implementation.

- **Without a repository configured:** each turn starts in a fresh temporary directory, with no project snapshot.
- **With `--repo` or configured `repo`:** each repository-backed turn gets a detached worktree of **committed `HEAD`**. Uncommitted edits and untracked dependencies such as `node_modules` are absent. Initial judging and continuation are transcript-only; final drafting can receive the repository.
- **Cleanup:** `finally` attempts worktree removal, then recursively removes the temporary directory. Worktree-removal errors are swallowed, potentially leaving Git metadata needing pruning. Crashes or cleanup exceptions prevent an unconditional cleanup guarantee.
- **Concurrency:** proposals run concurrently, as do reactions within each round. Rounds are sequential and share a prior-round transcript snapshot. Only Git worktree add/remove operations are serialized.

**Timeouts apply to individual CLI invocations**, normally ten minutes, configurable through `timeoutMinutes`. The adapter starts a detached process group, sends group `SIGTERM` at timeout, then `SIGKILL` after two seconds, and reports a returned timeout failure. This is not a whole-debate deadline and excludes Git setup/cleanup and artifact I/O. Processes escaping the group or remote work are not guaranteed stopped; completion still depends on child error/close events (`src/adapters.ts:8–59`; `src/index.ts:188`).

For **returned `{ok:false}` results**, assuming artifact writes succeed:

| Failure | Subsequent behavior and artifacts | Fresh-run CLI outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer than two proposals succeed | Aborts before reactions or judging. Saves partial `debate.md` and per-turn JSON under `raw/`. With your two debaters, either proposal failing triggers this. | Prints `debate.md`; exit **1**. |
| A reaction fails after successful proposals | Records failure in the transcript; later prompts receive a “no contribution” placeholder. Remaining rounds continue. With no judge, no determination or final report is created. | Prints `debate.md`; exit **0**. |
| Optional configured judge fails | Records failure and writes its failure message to `respondeo.md`; skips drafting. Transcript/raw artifacts remain. | Prints `debate.md`; exit **0**. |
| Final drafting fails after resolution | Retains the successful determination, transcript and failed-turn raw data; no new `final-report.md`. | Prints `debate.md`; exit **0**. |
